# DST Codex Umbra for Everyone

[English](#english) · [Português](#português) · [简体中文](#简体中文) · [Русский](#русский)

## English

A Don't Starve Together mod that lets every character craft and use Maxwell's Codex Umbra. It absorbs the former [Codex Umbra for Wortox](https://github.com/Lmedeiros-leiman/DST-CodexUmbraForWortox), which is no longer maintained.

- **Same rules as Maxwell:** shadow workers and protectors, shadow trap and pillars, nightmare fuel per spell, a max sanity penalty per shadow, and cleanup when the caster dies, becomes a ghost, swaps character or travels between the surface and the caves.
- **Shadows are silhouettes of their caster**, coloured by allegiance: none keeps Maxwell's black, Lunar glows gestalt cyan, Shadow turns void violet. Wormwood, a lunar plant creature, always gets the lunar colour. Characters with their own pets (Walter, Wormwood, WX-78) keep them; shadows use separate pet limits.
- **Every character has their own lines** about the book, the empty book, the "my mind can't take more" failure, their own shadow and someone else's shadow, in English, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Russian. See [LINES.md](LINES.md) for the full table.
- **Maxwell** plays exactly as in the base game. **Wortox** colours his shadows by his skill tree lean and allegiance, has his own rhyming lines, and summons a troupe of shadow dancers when he plays the panflute while carrying the Codex. **Wes** uses the book in silence. Modded characters get the book too, but are not guaranteed to work.
- **Allowed to cast shadow magic:** one switch per character (Maxwell included) plus one for modded characters, all on by default. A banned character can neither craft nor open the Codex and keeps their vanilla Codex line; a banned Maxwell loses his own shadow magic (magician recipes included) and grumbles about it.
- **Panflute Troupe** option: on / off.
- **Language** option: Auto (server language), English, Português, 简体中文, Русский.

## Português

Mod de Don't Starve Together que deixa todos os personagens criarem e usarem o Codex Umbra do Maxwell. Incorpora o antigo [Codex Umbra para o Wortox](https://github.com/Lmedeiros-leiman/DST-CodexUmbraForWortox), que não é mais mantido.

- **Mesmas regras do Maxwell:** servos e duelistas das sombras, armadilha e prisão sombrias, combustível de pesadelo por feitiço, penalidade de sanidade máxima por sombra, e as sombras somem quando quem as invocou morre, vira fantasma, troca de personagem ou viaja entre a superfície e as cavernas.
- **As sombras são silhuetas de quem as invoca**, coloridas pela aliança: nenhuma mantém o preto do Maxwell, Lunar brilha em ciano de gestalt, Sombra fica violeta do vazio. O Wormwood, uma criatura lunar, sempre tem a cor Lunar. Personagens com bichinhos próprios (Walter, Wormwood, WX-78) continuam com eles; as sombras usam limites separados.
- **Cada personagem tem falas próprias** sobre o livro, o livro vazio, a falha de "minha mente não aguenta mais", a própria sombra e a sombra de outro jogador, em inglês, português (Brasil), chinês simplificado e russo. A tabela completa está em [LINES.md](LINES.md).
- O **Maxwell** continua como no jogo base. O **Wortox** colore as sombras conforme a inclinação e a aliança da árvore de habilidades, tem falas próprias em rima e invoca uma trupe de sombras dançarinas ao tocar a flauta de pã carregando o Codex. O **Wes** usa o livro em silêncio. Personagens de mods também recebem o livro, sem garantia de funcionar.
- **Podem usar magia de sombra:** uma chave por personagem (inclusive o Maxwell) e uma para personagens de mods, todas ligadas por padrão. Quem for bloqueado não cria nem abre o Codex e mantém a fala original sobre ele; o Maxwell bloqueado perde a própria magia de sombra (inclusive as receitas de mágico) e reclama disso.
- Opção **Trupe da Flauta**: ligada / desligada.
- Opção **Idioma**: Automático (idioma do servidor), English, Português, 简体中文, Русский.

## 简体中文

让《饥荒联机版》的所有角色都能制作并使用麦斯威尔的暗影秘典。本模组整合了已停止维护的[沃拓克斯的暗影秘典](https://github.com/Lmedeiros-leiman/DST-CodexUmbraForWortox)。

- **规则与麦斯威尔相同：**暗影仆人和暗影角斗士、暗影陷阱和暗影囚牢，施法消耗噩梦燃料，每个暗影傀儡降低理智上限。
- **暗影是施法者的剪影**，颜色取决于阵营：无阵营为麦斯威尔的黑色，月亮为发光的虚影青色，暗影为虚空紫。
- **每个角色都有专属台词**，支持英语、巴西葡萄牙语、简体中文和俄语。完整表格见 [LINES.md](LINES.md)。
- 麦斯威尔与原版相同；沃拓克斯的暗影颜色取决于技能树的倾向和阵营，携带暗影秘典时吹奏排箫会召唤暗影舞者；韦斯默默使用这本书。
- **允许使用暗影魔法：**每个角色（包括麦斯威尔）以及模组角色都有单独的开关，默认全部开启。

## Русский

Мод для Don't Starve Together: все персонажи могут создавать и использовать Кодекс Умбра Максвелла. Включает в себя прежний мод [«Кодекс Умбра для Вортокса»](https://github.com/Lmedeiros-leiman/DST-CodexUmbraForWortox), который больше не поддерживается.

- **Правила те же, что у Максвелла:** теневые слуги и партнёры, теневые уловка и клетка, топливо кошмара за заклинание, штраф к максимальному рассудку за каждую тень.
- **Тени — силуэты призвавшего**, окрашенные по союзу: нет — чёрный, как у Максвелла, лунный — светящийся голубой, как у гештальта, теневой — фиолетовый цвет пустоты.
- **У каждого персонажа свои реплики** на английском, португальском (Бразилия), упрощённом китайском и русском. Полная таблица — в [LINES.md](LINES.md).
- Максвелл играет как в оригинале; тени Вортокса окрашены по наклонностям и союзу в древе навыков, а игра на флейте Пана с Кодексом при себе призывает теневых танцоров; Вэс пользуется книгой молча.
- **Могут использовать теневую магию:** отдельный переключатель для каждого персонажа (включая Максвелла) и для персонажей из модов, по умолчанию всё включено.

## Development

```bash
lua5.1 test/test_addon.lua            # lines, casters, options and an integration test with the engine
lua5.1 test/test_engine.lua           # shadow engine, tints and Wortox's features
python3 -I tools/build_lines.py drafts codex_everyone_lines.lua LINES.md
```

`codex_everyone_lines.lua` and `LINES.md` are generated from the drafts in `drafts/` (one JSON file per character with the lines in four languages and literal English meanings).
