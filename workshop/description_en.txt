[h1]Codex Umbra for Everyone[/h1]
Now every character can craft and use Maxwell's Codex Umbra, and Wortox gets his own colours, lines and a panflute troupe.

This item used to be Codex Umbra for Wortox. Everything it did is now part of this mod, so there is nothing else to subscribe to.

[h2]The Codex for everyone[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every character crafts the Codex Umbra from Maxwell's shadow tab and uses it exactly like Maxwell: shadow workers, shadow protectors, shadow trap and shadow pillars.
[*]Same rules as Maxwell: spells cost nightmare fuel and each shadow lowers max sanity (15% per worker, 90% cap). Shadows vanish when their caster dies, becomes a ghost, swaps character or travels between the surface and the caves.
[*]Characters with their own pets (Walter's Woby, Wormwood's plants, WX-78's drones) keep them: shadows have their own limits.
[/list]

[h2]Shadows in your colours[/h2]
[list]
[*]Shadows are silhouettes of their caster, wearing their skin.
[*]Their colour follows the caster's allegiance: none keeps Maxwell's black, Lunar glows gestalt cyan, Shadow turns void violet. Wormwood, a lunar plant creature, is always lunar.
[*]Wortox's shadows follow his skill tree instead: Nice is rose, Neutral crimson, Naughty a dark blood red. Lunar allegiance turns them glowing cyan, Shadow darkens them toward wine and violet.
[/list]

[h2]Everyone has something to say[/h2]
Every character has their own lines about the Codex, the empty Codex, a mind too crowded for more shadows, their own shadow and someone else's shadow, in English, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese and Russian. Some lines even change with the shadow owner's gender, and a few pick between two lines at random. Maxwell has opinions about amateurs performing his act.

[h2]Panflute Troupe[/h2]
Wortox plays the panflute while carrying the Codex Umbra and three shadow dancers in his colour appear around him for a short show. Purely cosmetic: no sanity cost, never saved, and they don't scare the wildlife.

[h2]Options[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Panflute Troupe[/b]: on / off.
[*][b]Language[/b]: Auto (server language), English, Português, 简体中文, Русский.
[*][b]Allowed to cast shadow magic[/b]: one switch per character, Maxwell included, plus one for modded characters. All on by default. A banned character can neither craft nor open the Codex and keeps their vanilla Codex line; a banned Maxwell loses his own shadow magic and is not happy about it.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Every player needs the mod.
[*]Maxwell plays as in the base game unless you ban him. Wes uses the book in silence.
[*]Modded characters get the book too, but are not guaranteed to work.
[*]Learning shadow magic also shows Maxwell's magician top hat and chest recipes; only Maxwell can use the top hat's magic.
[/list]
